The mission

Check site progress automatically — and prove every answer

On every project, someone reports progress and someone else has to check it. Right now a person does this by hand, clicking through site photos hundreds of times a week.

Your team builds an AI tool that reads these photos and gives an answer for each claim, with the exact image that proves it. If the photo does not show enough to decide, the tool should say so. A confident wrong guess loses points. Being honest earns them.

What you build

Track A, then the bonus round

Everyone builds Track A. It is the main challenge. Track B is extra points, only if you finish Track A first. A team can win on Track A alone.

Track A · required

Verify the claim sheet

Rohbau3D · 360° laser-scan panoramas · 8 German sites

Each scene comes with a list of claims — things a contractor might report (“walls fully plastered”, “windows installed”). About half are false. A few cannot be answered from the photo. For each claim, your tool gives a verdict and the image that proves it.

What changed here?

ConSLAM · one London project, photographed 4 times (Apr–Aug 2022)

The same areas were photographed on each visit. For one area and two visits, your tool reports what work was done in between. Each change is backed by a photo from both visits. This adds bonus points on top of Track A.

Get started — before event day

Four steps to building

  1. Clone the repo and read the docs. The README, DATA.md and SUBMISSION.md explain everything. Ten minutes in DATA.md saves an hour later. 
    git clone https://github.com/wakecap/contech-hackathon-2026
cd contech-hackathon-2026
  2. The photo packs come on a USB stick at the venue. No download needed on the day. Copy both zip files from the USB into the repo and run: 
    python3 scripts/download_data.py
    (It finds the zip files and unpacks them. You are building in about 2 minutes.)
  3. Build. Any method is allowed — vision-language models, classic computer vision, or a mix. There is no starter model on purpose. Finding the right method is part of the challenge.
  4. Check your file, then submit by pull request. You submit one submission.json file. Check it before the deadline — a format error wastes your build time. 
    python3 scripts/check_submission.py my_submission.json

At the end

The live test

During the pitches, the judges pick a scene (or an area pair for bonus teams) and ask your tool live: “How done is this? Prove it.”

Your tool answers on screen. Then the correct answer is shown right after. Build for this moment: an answer you trust, with the image behind it.

Ground rules

The rules

→
Teams of up to 5. Build on the day. Looking at the data before the event is expected and encouraged.
→
Every verdict needs a cited image. no_evidence is a valid answer that earns points. Be honest, not overconfident.
→
Both datasets are public, so you can read their papers. The claims and answers are written for this event, and they are what you are scored on.
→
Not needed: dashboards, cost tracking, payments, forecasting, viewers. We are judging the proof, not the dashboard.
